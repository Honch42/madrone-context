            Contexts: where notes are saved

            A context is an area of life or work with its own notes: its own Master Dossier, sessions, people and projects. Each one is a folder, either a whole Obsidian vault or a folder inside one. Pick the context in the top bar before a session. If you have more than one Google account connected, choose which ones apply to each context, so an interview never pulls in email from an unrelated area.
              Turn ends after silence
              Seconds of silence before your answer is sent. Press Space to send sooner.

              What this app can see

              Microphone
              Only during a session. Each answer is sent to Gemini for transcription; with a Gemini model, the interviewer hears it too.
              Camera
              Only during a session, and only if allowed. The video goes to your recordings folder. With a Gemini model it is uploaded to Google for analysis after the session and deleted from Google when the analysis returns.
              Claude and GPT
              Receive the transcript of what you said, never the audio or video. They can read notes in your notes folder when you mention one, but cannot change anything.
              Google accounts
              Read-only. Upcoming calendar events; subjects, senders and preview snippets of recent, starred, draft and deadline-related email; names of recently modified Drive files. Nothing is sent, edited or deleted. Used only when you ask ("what's coming up", "catch me up"), and only the accounts assigned to the active context if you've connected more than one.
              Inbox folders
              Read only during an inbox review, and only when you start one. Voice memos are sent to Gemini for transcription; reviewed files are moved to a Processed folder inside the inbox, never deleted.
              Screenpipe
              Read-only, and only when you ask. Recent window titles, screen text and heard speech from your local Screenpipe database.
              1Password
              Only when you click a 1Password button, and only after you approve 1Password's own prompt. The app sees item titles to build the list, and reads one item's secret when you choose it.
              Your Master Dossier
              Included in the interviewer's instructions so it can ask informed questions.
              Your notes and recordings
              Plain files in the folders above, protected only by your Mac's disk encryption and whatever syncs that folder. They hold personal reflections, so they are more sensitive than any key; keep the folder somewhere you trust.
              Everything else
              Stays on this Mac. Keys are stored encrypted in your Keychain. The app's internal server accepts connections only from this computer.
              Forgetting removes every key, Google connection and preference from this app. Your notes and recordings are left where they are.

              Before you connect a Google account

              What Madrone will read (all read-only):

              • Upcoming calendar events for the next 7 days.
              • Subjects, senders and preview snippets of recent, starred, draft and deadline-related email.
              • Names of recently modified Drive files.

              It never sends, edits or deletes anything, and it only looks when you ask during an interview. What it reads goes into that interview's questions and your session note on this Mac, nowhere else.
